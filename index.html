Projects

Academic major project · Team of 3 · Published

Smart City Traffic Forecasting

Forecasts hourly traffic at 4 city junctions up to 4 months ahead from 48,120 sensor records, with a Streamlit dashboard for congestion alerts.

  • Per-junction growth trend combined with Gradient Boosting on cyclical calendar features
  • Found and fixed target leakage; validated with 3 rolling-origin backtests
  • R² 0.87, 16% lower error than Linear Regression and 10% lower than a seasonal-naive baseline
  • Python
  • Scikit-learn
  • XGBoost
  • Plotly
  • SHAP
  • Streamlit

Personal project

Hospital Equipment RUL Prediction

Predicts the remaining useful life of hospital equipment so maintenance can be planned before a breakdown, with a Streamlit app that shows a risk level and explains each prediction.

  • OLS p-values, VIF and backward elimination narrowed 25 attributes to 3 real drivers
  • Compared 6 models with 5-fold CV; tuned Gradient Boosting with GridSearchCV
  • Error down from 0.89 years (age-only baseline) to 0.016 years on a simulated 50,000-record dataset
  • Python
  • Scikit-learn
  • Statsmodels
  • SHAP
  • Streamlit
